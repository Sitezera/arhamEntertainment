02 — Services

What we actually do

Strategy Production Social Talent Live Performance

Before anything gets made, we work out what the thing actually is and who it is for. That means audience research that goes past a deck of personas, a read on where the culture is heading between now and release, and a campaign platform strong enough to survive contact with a twelve-week calendar.

Audience research Campaign platform Release architecture Messaging framework Competitive landscape

A full in-house film and design team — directors, editors, designers, motion artists — set up to work at release speed. Trailers on Monday, twelve vertical cutdowns by Thursday, key art that survives being shrunk to a thumbnail on a phone.

Trailers & teasers Key art Motion design Vertical social Photography Sound design

Channels run by people who watch the show, follow the team, know the album. Daily publishing, reactive posting when something breaks, and moderation that holds up when a launch week brings ten thousand comments an hour.

Channel strategy Always-on publishing Community management Reactive content Reporting

We cast creators the way you'd cast a film — fit first, follower count second. Contracts, briefs, usage rights and disclosure all handled in-house, so a campaign never derails over paperwork nobody read.

Creator casting Talent partnerships Press junkets Usage & rights Disclosure compliance

Premieres, festival builds, fan events and press moments. Every activation is designed to work twice — once for the few hundred people in the room, and once for the few million who will only ever see it as a fifteen-second clip.

Premieres Festival activations Fan events Set & stage build Content capture

How it runs

Five steps, no mystery

01

Immersion

We watch it, read it, listen to it. A week inside the material before anyone writes a line of strategy.

02

The Idea

One platform, written on one page. If it needs a deck to explain, it isn't finished.

03

Build

Production and channel setup run in parallel, on a calendar pinned to your release date and nothing else.

04

Launch

A live room for the full window. Daily reads, fast calls, and the freedom to change the plan when the audience tells us to.

05

The Read

An honest post-mortem within two weeks. What worked, what didn't, and what we'd spend differently next time.
