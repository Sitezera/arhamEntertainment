How we operate

Four house rules

01

One idea, one page

If the platform needs forty slides to land, it isn't a platform — it's a list. We write the idea on one page or we go back and find a better one.

02

Make it, don't brief it

Everything is produced in-house. No middle layer, no handoff to a third party who has never met the client — the people who pitched it are the people who cut it.

03

Numbers we'd defend

We report the metrics that map to what you actually wanted — tickets, subscriptions, trial, renewals. Reach is context, not a result.

04

Say the awkward thing

If the release date is wrong, the trailer isn't working, or the budget won't cover the ambition, you hear it from us early — not in the post-mortem.

How we got here

Twelve years, abridged

2014

One room, one client

Founded above a photo lab in Lower Parel with a single independent film account and a borrowed edit suite.

2017

Production comes in-house

Hired the first three-person film team after one too many campaigns stalled waiting on an external edit.

2019

First festival account

The Harbour Festival brought us on for on-sale. It sold out before the paid media even went live.

2022

Sport and streaming

Expanded past film into league marketing and streaming launches — the same playbook, a longer calendar.

2026

Twenty-six people

Strategy, production, social, talent and live — all under one roof, still in Lower Parel, two floors up from where we started.
